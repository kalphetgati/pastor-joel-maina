About Pastor Joel

A Servant of God with a Vision

Pastor Joel Maina is a dedicated servant of God called to mentor, guide, and counsel individuals through life's journey. With years of experience in ministry, he has touched countless lives through the transformative power of God's Word.

He currently serves as Pastor at JCC Kahawa West Church, where he is actively involved in teaching, mentorship, and community outreach alongside the pastoral team.

His ministry focuses on Bible-based counseling for families, marriages, business professionals, youth, teens and those seeking spiritual and emotional healing and restoration. Pastor Joel believes in the power of mentorship to shape generations and build communities rooted in Christ and faith in God.
